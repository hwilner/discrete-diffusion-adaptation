**Headline:** 20x Faster Image Generation: A Deep Dive into Emu3.5's DiDA Mechanism

**Body:**

Excited to share my latest project: a from-scratch PyTorch implementation of **Discrete Diffusion Adaptation (DiDA)**, the groundbreaking inference technique from BAAI's Emu3.5 paper!

DiDA is the magic behind Emu3.5's incredible efficiency, accelerating image generation by approximately 20x without sacrificing quality. It achieves this by transforming the standard token-by-token autoregressive process into a parallel, bidirectional one.

I wanted to go beyond just reading the paper and truly understand how this is possible. This implementation is the result: a clean, educational, and fully-tested version of the DiDA mechanism, designed for anyone who wants to explore the future of efficient image generation.

**Key Features of the Repository:**

- **🧠 Clear & Commented Code:** Written with Google-style docstrings and a focus on readability.
- **✅ Comprehensive Tests:** 18 unit and integration tests ensure the correctness of the implementation (validated with synthetic data).
- **📚 Detailed Documentation:** Includes a deep dive into the DiDA architecture and testing methodology.
- **🚀 Working Examples:** Demonstrates how to use the implementation for both pure image generation and text-to-image tasks.

This project was a fantastic learning experience, and I'm thrilled to share it with the community. Understanding these fundamental building blocks is key to unlocking the next wave of AI innovation.
